About

Still early in the climb — and building the habits that make cloud infrastructure reliable.

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science Engineering student at GNA University, specializing in Cloud Computing and DevOps. My interest started with wanting to know what actually happens after a developer runs git push — that curiosity pulled me into Linux, AWS, containers, and the pipelines that move code from a laptop to production.

I'm not presenting myself as a seasoned DevOps engineer — I'm a student who learns by building. I hold the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner certification and completed Red Hat System Administration I, and I'm currently working toward the AWS Solutions Architect – Associate certification.

Right now I'm looking for a DevOps or Cloud internship in the Delhi NCR region, where I can take that hands-on foundation and keep growing it under people who've done this for real.
